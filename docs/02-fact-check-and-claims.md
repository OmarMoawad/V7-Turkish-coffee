# 02 — Fact-Check & Claims Policy

A heritage brand lives or dies on trust. Several beliefs in the founder's original write-up are
wrong or overstated. None of them weakens the product; they weaken the pitch if left in. This doc
corrects them and sets the rules for what may appear on a menu, a pack or a pitch deck. It follows
the same doctrine as `vcola-tech-strategy` Doc 30.

## 1. Corrections

| Original belief | What the evidence says | How to say it instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Over 80% of the world's coffee is Robusta" | Robusta is roughly **30–40%** of world output (USITC); its share has been rising and was about 42% in 2022 on USITC's figures. Arabica is the majority | "Made only from Arabica, which is the finer and majority species" |
| "Robusta is a fake Chinese copy" | Robusta (*Coffea canephora*) is a separate species, grown mainly in Vietnam, Brazil and Africa. Arabica itself descends from a natural cross that includes *canephora* | Don't attack Robusta. Sell what Yemeni Arabica *is* |
| "Yemeni Arabica is the parent of all coffee" | Arabica originated in **Ethiopia**; coffee was carried to Yemen and cultivated there. Yemen's port of **Mocha** was the first great coffee export port, so Yemen is the birthplace of the coffee **trade** and cultivation culture | "From the land that first cultivated and traded coffee" |
| "Grown at ≥ 1,000 ft" | Yemen's main regions (Haraz, Bani Matar, Hayma, Dhamar) are at roughly **1,700–2,400 m** (≈ 5,600–7,900 ft) | Specify ≥ 1,700 m |
| "Yemeni is 8–10× the price of everyday coffee" | Roughly right as a range. Specialty Yemeni green sells around **$65–68/kg FOB** versus benchmark Arabica near **$7/kg** and Robusta near **$3.7/kg** (mid-2026); top auction lots go far higher | Keep, but cite the number, not a slogan |
| "Only Egyptian sugar uses sugarcane" | Egypt produces **more beet sugar than cane**: about 1.8 Mt beet vs 1.0 Mt cane in 2023 | Specify **cane sugar** explicitly, and verify the supplier |
| "Saffron prevents caffeine anxiety; awake 7am–9pm without anxiety" | A 2019 meta-analysis (23 trials) found saffron supplements at ~30 mg/day may reduce depression and anxiety symptoms, but most trials came from one country, many from one group, with publication bias. A culinary pinch is about the same order of magnitude, but there's **no evidence it offsets caffeine**. Caffeine's half-life is ~5 hours; "alert until 9pm" isn't a defensible claim | No health claims. "Saffron, the world's most prized spice" |
| "Mastic has very high medicinal benefits" | Chios mastic is genuinely special: EU **PDO since 1997**, produced only in southern Chios. Medicinal claims are regulated health claims | "Chios mastic (PDO), hand-harvested on one Greek island" |
| "Pistachio is good for the heart" | Plausible as general nutrition, but a regulated claim on a menu | No claim. Describe it as a crunchy finish |
| "The version served to the Ottoman Sultan, per rumours" | Not documentable | "Inspired by Ottoman court and Cairo coffeehouse traditions" |
| "Cairene scientists were the building blocks of the Ottoman empire" | Overstated. What's defensible: Cairo was one of the earliest great coffeehouse cities, and coffee culture reached Istanbul from the Arab lands in the 16th century | "Shaped by Cairo's oldest coffeehouses" |

## 2. Claims we can make confidently

- **Turkish coffee culture and tradition** is on UNESCO's Representative List of Intangible
  Cultural Heritage (inscribed 2013). UNESCO describes the method as finely ground fresh-roasted
  coffee with cold water and sugar, heated slowly until foam forms. That is close to this method.
- 100% high-altitude Yemeni Arabica; light roast; ground fresh for every pot.
- Chios mastic (PDO); Persian saffron (state grade, e.g. Super Negin, once sourced).
- Eight years of development, informed by interviews with Cairo's long-established coffeehouses.
- Hand-brewed on the lowest flame, one pot at a time.

## 3. Banned implications

- Any disease, anxiety, heart or "medicinal" claim, explicit or implied.
- "The Sultan's exact recipe", "secret", "forbidden", "the original".
- "No added sugar" or "healthy" (it contains 27 g of sugar per pot).
- Disparaging other coffees or species.

## 4. The rule

No claim reaches a menu, label, social post or pitch until it has **a source in
[`references.md`](references.md)** or a **dated test log** behind it. The founder signs off; when
the brand grows, a small advisory panel does (same model as the V7 provenance board).
