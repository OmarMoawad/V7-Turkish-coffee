# 08 — Go-to-Market

The goal of the first year isn't revenue maximisation. It's **proof**: a blind-tested, paid-for,
repeat-purchased premium heritage coffee with a recognisable name.

## 1. Formats, in launch order

| # | Format | Why first / later | Capex |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weekend tasting counter** in a premium venue (café residency, hotel lobby, curated market) | Tests demand and price cheaply; no lease | Very low |
| 2 | **Home blend** (250 g + mastic/saffron sachets), sold at the counter and via Instagram/WhatsApp orders | Scalable; doesn't need brewer-hours | Low |
| 3 | **Private ceremonies**: hosting at homes, Ramadan and Eid gatherings, corporate events, weddings | Highest price per hour; strong word of mouth | Low |
| 4 | **Permanent counter** (small footprint, 6–10 seats) | Only after Gate 3 | Medium |
| 5 | **Hospitality licensing**: a trained brewer and the blend placed in a luxury hotel | Brand reach without own rent | Low–medium |
| 6 | **Gulf pop-up and export blend** | After Gate 5 | Medium |

## 2. Cairo first

- **Where:** upscale districts and venues where customers already pay specialty prices, plus
  hotels that serve tourists looking for an authentic experience.
- **When:** Ramadan and Eid are natural peaks for hosting and gifting; plan the first ceremony
  season around them.
- **Who:** quality-driven Turkish coffee drinkers, gift buyers, hosts, visitors and diaspora.

## 3. The Gulf next

Gulf buyers already pay for Yemeni coffee, saffron and cardamom, and Egyptian roasters
(Entro) and Saudi roasters bid on top Yemeni auction lots. A Gulf pop-up tests the export price
ladder (Doc 05 §4) and is the bridge to V7's own 80-market export ambition.

## 4. Brand and story

- **Name:** test a few options; something that carries Cairo + Yemen + craft. Register the
  trademark before public launch (Doc 06).
- **Story, in order:** the taste first; then the eight-year quest; then Yemen as coffee's first
  trade origin; then Cairo's coffeehouse heritage. All within the claims rules (Doc 02).
- **Service ritual:** cezve brought to the table, the pour, the foam, water alongside (as UNESCO
  describes the tradition), pistachios on the side, a short story card.

## 5. Channels for word of mouth

- Short videos of the slow brew and the foam (the process *is* the content).
- Invite-only tastings for coffee writers, food creators and hotel F&B managers.
- Publish the blind-test result once it exists: "ranked first in a blind test against X and Y".

## 6. What not to do in year one

- No espresso menu, no lattes, no pastry programme.
- No discounting to chain-café prices.
- No health claims, no "Sultan's recipe" claims (Doc 02).
- No second location before Gate 4.
