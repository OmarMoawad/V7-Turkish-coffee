# 09 — Sourcing & Supply Chain

The cup is only as good, and as defensible, as its inputs. Yemen is the hardest one.

## 1. Yemeni coffee

**What to buy:** specialty-grade Yemeni Arabica from the high-altitude northwest regions —
Haraz, Bani Matar, Hayma, Dhamar — roughly **1,700–2,400 m**. These are commonly sold as
"Mocha Harazi" or "Mocha Mattari".

**Price reference:**
- Specialty export lots: about **$65–68/kg FOB** (TYPICA listing).
- Best of Yemen 2025 auction: weighted average **$137.08/lb**, top lot **$851.50/lb**. Egypt's
  Entro Coffee was among the leading buyers. You don't need auction lots for the Signature cup;
  a small auction lot could be a limited "Grand Reserve" later.

**Risks and mitigations:**

| Risk | Detail | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Conflict and transport | A road trip from Sana'a to Aden that once took ~10 hours can now take two days or more; a shipment may be a farm's entire season | Buy through established exporters; hold 4–6 months of green stock |
| Sanctions | Much Yemeni coffee is grown in Houthi-controlled areas; the 2025 US terrorist redesignation raises compliance risk for buyers | Use exporters with documented compliance; take legal advice before any US-linked trade or export |
| Fraud | "Yemeni" is a premium label that invites blending with cheaper beans | Buy traceable lots; cup every batch; keep samples |
| Price swings | Global coffee prices have been volatile | Fix prices per harvest where possible; menu prices reviewed quarterly |

**Roasting:** start with a trusted Cairo specialty roaster under NDA using your profile, or a
small shop roaster (1–2 kg) once volume justifies it. Roast date on every bag.

## 2. Cardamom

Highest-grade green cardamom (large, bright green pods, high oil content; India and Guatemala
are the main origins). Grind pods with the coffee or immediately before blending; ground
cardamom loses aroma fast.

## 3. Chios mastic

Buy **PDO Chios mastic**, protected by the EU since 1997 and produced only in the mastic villages
of southern Chios. Buy from the growers' association or certified sellers; check the PDO label.

## 4. Persian saffron

Specify the grade (e.g. Super Negin or Sargol) and buy from a seller with lab test certificates.
Saffron fraud (dyed threads, added stamens) is common. Wholesale Iranian Super Negin was about
$1.9/g in early 2025. Note import rules and payment constraints when buying Iranian products.

## 5. Pistachios

"Aleppo" (Syrian) pistachios are famous but may be hard to source reliably; **Antep (Gaziantep,
Türkiye)** pistachios are the standard premium alternative with a similar profile. Roast lightly
in-house; store airtight.

## 6. Sugar and water

- **Cane sugar**, verified as cane. Most Egyptian sugar is now beet (Doc 02).
- Filtered drinking water with consistent mineral content; test one brand against your tap filter
  in blind trials and fix it in the spec.
