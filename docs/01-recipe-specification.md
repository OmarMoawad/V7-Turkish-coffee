# 01 — The Recipe Specification

The method exists today mostly in one person's hands. The first job is to turn it into a
**controlled specification**: every parameter named, its current value written down, and a note
on whether it's proven by testing or still a belief. This is the document that Doc 06 protects
and Doc 07 tests.

> Version: **v1.0 (as stated by the founder, Nov 2026).** Values marked *untested* come from
> research, not from the founder's own trials.

## 1. Ingredients

| Ingredient | Specification v1.0 | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee | 100% Yemeni Arabica, single origin or Yemeni blend | Proven (founder trials) |
| Altitude | Founder's draft said "≥ 1,000 ft". **Correct to ≥ 1,700 m**: Yemen's main regions sit at roughly 1,700–2,400 m (Doc 02) | Correction |
| Roast | Light roast | Proven |
| Rest after roasting | *Not yet specified.* Recommended 2–5 days (Doc 03) | To test |
| Grind | Turkish-fine powder, ground immediately before brewing | Proven |
| Cardamom | Highest-grade green cardamom, **coffee : cardamom = 9 : 1 by weight** | Proven |
| Dose | 18–24 g of the coffee + cardamom mix per pot; **pure coffee never below 18 g** | Proven |
| Sugar | 27 g cane sugar per pot (sweet). Offer sugar levels (Doc 03) | Proven / to extend |
| Water | Good-quality drinking water, refrigerated overnight (fridge-cold, not iced, not room temp) | Proven |
| Ice | 1–3 small ice cubes added at the start | Proven by founder; mechanism unclear (Doc 03) |
| Variant A | Chios mastic (PDO), a few crushed tears per pot | *Untested* — dosage to set |
| Variant B | Persian saffron, a few threads, ideally bloomed | *Untested* — dosage to set |
| Side | Lightly roasted pistachios (Aleppo or Antep), **served on the side, not in the pot** | Recommendation (Doc 03) |

## 2. Equipment

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Vessel | Copper cezve, double size, for 200–250 ml |
| Heat | Gas stove, **smallest burner at its lowest setting**. Not a sand stove, not electric |
| Stirring | By hand, ~1,000 strokes during the slow heat-up |

## 3. Procedure (as stated)

1. Weigh 18–24 g of freshly ground coffee + cardamom (9:1) and 27 g sugar.
2. Add fridge-cold water (200–250 ml) and 1–3 small ice cubes to the cezve.
3. Add coffee and sugar.
4. Place on the smallest gas burner at the lowest flame.
5. Stir by hand ~1,000 times while it heats slowly; the foam (*wesh*) builds gradually.
6. Pour as the foam rises, before a full boil.

## 4. What to measure from now on

The founder's eight years of tuning live in feel and judgement. To preserve and transfer it
(Doc 06), log these for every test pot:

| Parameter | How to measure | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Water temperature at start | Probe thermometer | Defines "fridge-cold" in numbers |
| Burner heat | Time to heat 200 ml of water from 4 °C to 90 °C | Makes "lowest flame" reproducible on any stove |
| Time to first foam / to pour | Stopwatch | The real control variable behind slow heating |
| Stir count and rate | Count or clicker | Tests whether 1,000 matters (Doc 03) |
| Grind size | Same grinder + setting; photo against a ruler | Consistency |
| Bean age | Days since roast | Freshness window |
| Foam score | 1–5 by two tasters | Quality signal |
| Taste score | Blind 9-point hedonic scale (Doc 07) | Quality signal |

## 5. Versioning

Every change gets a new version number and a dated test log. **Never change two variables at
once.** v1.0 is the baseline everything is compared against.
