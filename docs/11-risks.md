# 11 — Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blind tests don't confirm superiority** | Medium | High | Gate 1 before spending; iterate on Doc 03 variables; accept a smaller boutique if it's "very good" but not "best" |
| **Founder dependency** (only one person can brew it) | High at start | High | Doc 06: spec, video, apprenticeship; Gate 4 before scaling |
| **Throughput ceiling** (manual, ~5 pots/brewer-hour) | Certain | Medium | Premium pricing; ceremony format; home blend as the scalable line; stir test |
| **Yemen supply disruption** (conflict, transport, sanctions) | Medium–High | High | 4–6 months of green stock; multiple exporters; legal check; a contingency "Arabian highland" blend disclosed honestly if Yemeni stock runs out |
| **Copycats** | Medium | Medium | Trade secrets, trademark, trained brewers, supplier ties, first-mover reputation |
| **Price resistance in Cairo** | Medium | Medium | Price tests at three levels; ceremony and gift formats; Gulf as higher-price market |
| **Currency moves** (EGP vs USD inputs) | High | Medium | Quarterly menu price review; forward buying of green coffee; Gulf revenue in hard currency |
| **Overclaiming** (health, "Sultan's recipe") | Medium | High (trust, legal) | Doc 02 claims policy; sign-off before anything public |
| **Food safety** (mastic/saffron fraud, contamination) | Low–Medium | High | Certified suppliers, lab certificates for saffron, PDO-labelled mastic, batch logs |
| **Founder bandwidth** (studies, PPDS, other projects) | High | High | Treat year one as a part-time proof: weekend counter, small batches; hire the first brewer early |
| **Stalled V7 conversation** | Medium | Medium | The coffee venture stands on its own; V7 is upside, not a dependency |
