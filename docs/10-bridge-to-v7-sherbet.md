# 10 — Bridge to the V7 Sherbet Line

This venture is a means as well as an end. The plan in `vcola-tech-strategy` (Docs 12–15) asks
V7 to trust an outside founder with a heritage sherbet category. A successful heritage coffee
venture makes that ask much stronger.

## 1. What the coffee venture proves to V7

| V7 needs to believe… | The coffee venture shows… |
|---|---|
| Historical research can become a product people love | Eight years of research → a blind-tested, preferred cup |
| Premium heritage pricing works in Egypt | Paid pilots at 2.5–3× chain-café prices with repeat purchase |
| Honest provenance can be a brand asset | A claims policy (Doc 02) and evidence-graded story in practice |
| The founder can execute, not just strategise | A running, profitable counter and a trained team |
| There is a ceremonial / on-site channel | The coffee counter becomes **Tier 3 (on-site fresh preparation)** from `vcola-tech-strategy` Doc 13 |

## 2. The natural product link

Coffee and sherbet were served together in Ottoman and Cairene hospitality. A heritage coffee
counter can add **2–3 sherbets** (e.g. tamarind, hibiscus, carob) as a small test menu:

- It gives V7's proposed sherbets a **real tasting room** before any bottling investment.
- It produces exactly the data Doc 12's Phase 0–1 needs: taste preference, price, repeat rate.
- It positions the counter as a **heritage beverage house**, not only a coffee shop.

## 3. How to pitch V7

1. Lead with evidence, not vision: blind-test results, paid-pilot repeat rates, revenue.
2. Offer a **low-risk co-brand pilot**: V7 sherbets served at the counter for one Ramadan season,
   instrumented the same way as the coffee.
3. Then propose the Doc 12 staged plan, with the counter as the on-site tier.
4. Keep the coffee brand **yours**. Co-branding is a partnership, not a transfer. Decide early
   what V7 gets (sherbet distribution rights, a licence, an equity link) and what stays with you.

## 4. Timing

- Don't pitch the coffee venture to V7 before Gate 3 (paid demand). Without paid data, it's
  another idea.
- Ideal moment: after a successful first season, with a Ramadan sherbet pilot proposal ready for
  the following Ramadan.
